# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

If an order has not arrived within 10 business days of the estimated delivery
date, customers are eligible for a full refund or a free reshipment, whichever
they prefer.

Lost packages should be reported to the carrier first, but if the customer
has already done so and the carrier confirms the package is lost, we issue
the refund/reshipment without further delay.

International orders can take up to 21 business days and are not eligible
for the 10-day guarantee above.
