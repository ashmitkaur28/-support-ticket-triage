# Product FAQ

Product warranty covers manufacturing defects for 12 months from purchase.
It does not cover damage from misuse or normal wear and tear.

Size/fit questions: refer customers to the size chart on the product page.
We do not offer phone consultations for sizing.

Product availability: if an item shows "out of stock," customers can sign
up for a restock notification email on the product page. We do not do
manual restock notifications via support.
