# Technical Issues

Website/app login errors: first ask the customer to clear cache or try
an incognito window, as this resolves ~60% of login issues.

Payment failures at checkout are usually caused by the card issuer's
fraud check, not our system. Advise the customer to contact their bank
if the card is valid and has sufficient funds.

Mobile app crashes should be logged with device type and app version;
escalate to engineering if more than isolated reports come in for the
same device/version combo.
