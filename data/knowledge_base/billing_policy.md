# Billing & Refunds Policy

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original payment method.

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase for unopened items,
and within 14 days for opened items showing a defect.

Duplicate charges should be verified against the order ID. If confirmed
duplicate, refund the extra charge immediately without requiring a return.

Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing
cycle; we do not offer partial-month refunds unless required by local law.
